TONGA 2022

We knew what to do. Because Tonga had already done it in 2022. The Hunga Tonga volcano erupts, the tsunami severs the undersea cable — Tonga loses internet entirely. The Ministry of Agriculture maps crop damage, coordinates recovery, computes relief payments. Fully offline. No license server to call home to. That solution was openly available to anyone. When Switzerland needed it two years later, we didn’t reinvent it. That’s what a digital public good does: replicate solutions, not problems. And every deployment makes the next one better. Switzerland didn’t just inherit Tonga’s solution — whoever comes next will inherit ours.
