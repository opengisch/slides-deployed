NEPAL FLOODS, AUGUST 2026

Everything offline

1:00. Last story, and unfortunately it’s Ticino all over again: floods, this time in Nepal, last month. And this time we knew even more. A totally offline QField project: OSM data as offline vector tiles you can still identify features on, 60 cm Vantor imagery from 24 hours earlier, and full 3D with the 30 m SRTM DEM as offline elevation. Say “3D, offline” out loud: it’s new since 4.1. Mathieu shared it in HOTOSM’s #disaster-mapping Slack channel, a little over 2,000 people from the private sector and non-profits.
